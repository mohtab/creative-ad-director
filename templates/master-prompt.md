# Master creative-ad production prompt template

Replace every placeholder before final delivery, or flag a genuinely missing asset explicitly. Do not force sections that do not apply. The generation section is pasteable into the selected model; exact finishing directives are for the editor, not guarantees of native generation.

## Approved brief — outside the generation prompt
- Brand/product and factual status:
- Audience / situation / objective:
- Single promise and observable proof:
- Creative route / campaign line / emotional arc:
- CTA and verified destination, if supplied:
- Placement / language / duration / deliverables:
- Reference assets and unresolved assumptions:

## Generation settings — verified against selected surface
Model/platform and mode; asset slots/identifiers; aspect ratio; supported native duration/resolution; native audio on/off or scratch-only purpose. Separate final export targets. State any unverified setting or limit.

## MASTER PROMPT

### CAMPAIGN AND SELLING IDEA
Create a [duration] advertisement for [brand/product]. Campaign: “[line]”. Make [audience] feel/understand [promise], demonstrated by [visible proof]. Follow [appropriate narrative or visual mechanism]. End with [action]. Tone: [specific complementary qualities].

### FORMAT, WORLD AND REFERENCE CONTRACT — GENERATE
Target [composition and production style]. Treat timestamps as pacing targets; exact edit timing is specified below.
Use [actual reference identifier] for [role]. Preserve [product identity/geometry, verified location/packaging/label attributes]. Allowed creative variation: [bounded freedom]. Never imply [unsupported factual features].
Hard priorities: [truth/identity], [continuity], [key selling moment], [CTA-compatible end composition].

### SUBJECT / PRODUCT / STATE LOCK — GENERATE
[Only if applicable: character appearance, wardrobe, props, count, motion axis, start/end state, expression arc. For product-only work: product finish, orientation, function and handling.]

### SHOT 01 | [IN–OUT seconds] | [SELLING FUNCTION]
Framing/lens guidance:
Camera placement and movement:
Primary action and visible product proof:
Composition/light/focus:
Continuity in -> out / sound bridge:
[Repeat for the chosen number of shots. No decorative shot without a reason.]

### GENERATION SOUND
[One unambiguous choice: native final audio OR ambience-only/scratch OR mute for post. No embedded VO request if final VO is separately added. Specify relevant diegetic cues.]

### VOICEOVER — EDIT / SEPARATE AUDIO
[Language/accent, human or product tone, performance direction, exact lines and timing windows. Omit when not useful. No lip-sync unless intended and supported.]

### MUSIC AND SOUND MIX — EDIT
[Original/licensed music direction; tempo/meter only if useful; build/payoff/resolve points; ambience and tactile sounds; transitions; narration clarity and music ducking; end decay.]

### BRANDING AND FINISH — EDIT
[Exact approved logo/text/CTA, introduction and hold times, safe placement, contrast, typography/RTL requirements, approved legal copy. Final aspect ratio, duration, FPS, frame count, resolution, upscale/conform needs. Cut and grade direction. No fake URL, claims or unapproved offer.]

### FAILURE PREVENTION AND ACCEPTANCE
[Short ranked constraints. Acceptance checks for the hero product, proof, state continuity, exact finish and CTA legibility. Repair/split-generation fallback where appropriate.]

## Handoff notes — outside the prompt
- What generation can approximate versus what editing must enforce.
- Missing references/approvals and optional per-shot fallback.
- Timing validation result, VO timing caveat and render status.
