# Creative discipline and generation limits

Give every shot a selling function. Build a clear promise, visible proof, payoff and invitation. Track product truth, identity, props and screen direction. Choose pacing and shot count for the idea, not an example campaign. Do not invent product claims, testimonials, prices or destinations.

A master prompt is a production treatment, not proof that generated footage satisfies it. Generate clean imagery where possible; add exact logos, typography, timing and final sound in editing. Inspect actual output before accepting continuity, duration, resolution or sound.

Platform is not model: two hosts exposing a named model may differ in modes, controls, billing and reference handling. Recheck official documentation and the selected interface before using any duration, resolution, reference or audio limit. Native generation and upscaled delivery are different. No platform-specific capability limit is guaranteed by this package.

If one-pass multishot output fails, repair or generate individual shots with handles and trim in editing. Keep selectors and prompt directions consistent. Preserve truthful brand geometry over decorative camera detail.

Read timings as editorial plans. The validator checks arithmetic, not rendered fidelity. Voiceover word-count warnings are rough, language-dependent estimates; a spoken performance is needed to confirm fit.
