# Fictional example: A place for the everyday

This is an original instructional concept, not a real product or proven campaign.

- Product: fictional compartmented desk tray; verify geometry from an approved asset before rendering.
- Audience: people seeking a tidier workspace.
- Promise: give everyday desk objects a clear place.
- Proof: show the same pen, notebook and keys placed in appropriate compartments.
- CTA: Explore the collection. No invented store link.
- Plan: 12 seconds, 24 fps; use assets/example-timeline.json for arithmetic validation.

Shots: 0–3 seconds establish the scattered objects; 3–6 show a single deliberate placement; 6–9 reveal the organized arrangement; 9–12 hold the product with open space for the CTA in post.

Keep counts and placement coherent. Do not imply measured productivity gains. This is a demonstration of planning, not a complete reference-locked prompt or a rendered advertisement.
